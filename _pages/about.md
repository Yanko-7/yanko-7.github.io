I am Yongkang Qi (漆永康), a computer science master's student at [East China Normal University](https://www.ecnu.edu.cn/), where I focus on **generative models for CAD** and **high-performance geometric modeling**. My earlier degree in Software Engineering is from South China Agricultural University, and I have a competitive-programming background (ACM-ICPC / CCPC medalist).

My research sits at the intersection of geometry and machine learning: generating valid, high-fidelity CAD **Boundary Representation (B-Rep)** models, verifiable reinforcement learning for image-to-CAD program synthesis, and robust discretization of complex B-Rep geometry. Alongside research, I work on ML infrastructure and inference optimization, and I contribute to open-source projects such as [sglang-jax](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang-jax), [Apache Doris](https://github.com/apache/doris), and [xmake](https://github.com/xmake-io/xmake-repo).

**Technical skills.** C/C++ (11/14/17/20), Python, Haskell, Rust, and TypeScript; PyTorch with a focus on deep-learning training and inference optimization; and ML-systems work spanning collective-communication primitives, Triton, and LLM serving infrastructure.
