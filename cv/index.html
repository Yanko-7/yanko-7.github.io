Projects

  • 2026 - 2026
    FlowAR-BRep (accepted at SIGGRAPH Asia 2026)

    Single-stage generative framework for CAD Boundary Representation (B-Rep) that jointly models discrete topology and continuous parametric geometry within one Transformer backbone.

    • Unified-modality architecture: encodes discrete topology and continuous geometry into a single interleaved sequence (Transfusion paradigm); drops lossy VQ-VAE discretization and generates parametric geometry directly with a Flow-Matching (x-prediction) head plus a classification head for topology tokens — end-to-end cross-modal training and autoregressive generation in one Transformer.
    • Constrained decoding & long-sequence optimization: a finite-state machine combined with graphics validation enables backtracking and resampling for validity; Sequence Packing with variable-length attention greatly improves training efficiency on 4x RTX 5090.
    • Multimodal & SOTA: supports point-cloud / image / text-conditioned generation; unconditional generation reaches SOTA on public benchmarks with markedly higher validity and ~2.8x inference speedup.
  • 2026 - 2026
    VeriCAD-RL

    Verifiable RL fine-tuning for multi-view image-to-CAD program generation.

    • Post-trained an instruction-tuned Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct with GRPO via the ms-swift framework to improve executability and geometric consistency of generated parametric CAD programs.
    • Built a CadQuery verifier with rewards from program execution, modeling-kernel validity checks, voxelized IoU, and point-cloud Chamfer Distance.
    • Raised test-set success rate 80.14% -> 88.75%, Mean IoU 0.521 -> 0.628, Median IoU 0.585 -> 0.735.
  • 2024 - Present
    GME — Geometric Modeling Engine

    High-performance discretization framework for complex B-Rep models (multi-university project with Tsinghua, XJTU, HDU, ECNU); robustly generates watertight triangle meshes and runs ~2.3x faster than ACIS on partners’ test sets. Deployed at Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding and Yunji.

    • Curvature-aware adaptive NURBS sampling that produces shape-preserving meshes under a geometric-error bound.
    • Hybrid Newton-Raphson + bisection solver for trimming-curve / mesh-line intersection in parameter space.
    • Robust topology repair for periodic surfaces, poles/singularities, discontinuous trim loops, and edge stitching to guarantee watertight output.
    • Arena Allocator to cut dynamic-allocation overhead; strategy-pattern mesh backend supporting multi-precision output.
